# Kill Clog Next Release

Target version: `2.3.0`

## Release Notes

Kill Clog 2.3.0

* shows duplicate quantities when hovering obtained collection log items
* turns all 24 skill tooltips into Collection Log summaries with activity sections, aggregate completion, and adaptive item sprites
* gives every combat skill a grounded progression, including Hitpoints equipment unlocks, defenders for Defence, and Strength weapon unlocks
* adds the full current Slayer Collection Log and Rifts Closed progress to their relevant skill tooltips
* makes main-grid skills the default, keeps their footprint before a lookup, matches boss-cell sizing, and leaves unsynced skill levels white
* lets synced players use their Skill Color, level-99 completion, or full K1-K4 Skill Clog progression
* simplifies the Skill Summary around total XP at rest and the hovered skill's XP and rank
* lets players hide the comparison control for a cleaner search bar
* puts killclog.com first in the reorganized settings and clarifies chat emoji help text
* uses full item naming in summary hovers, including Tumeken's Shadow
* makes first-time Collection Log setup explicit and completes boss and clue-tier support for `!kclog`, `!missing`, and RuneProfile-compatible `!log` commands
