# Kill Clog

Boss KC tracker with collection log integration for RuneLite.

Look up any player. See every boss kill, collection log progress, and account milestone in one panel. Sync your own clog in-game and watch the numbers light up.

![sync-path](screenshots/sync-path.gif)

Open your collection log. Click the trophy. Done.

## Panel

![panel](screenshots/panel.png)

65 bosses. Color-coded by collection log completion. One glance tells you where you stand.

## Configuration

![config](screenshots/config.png)

- **Auto-Lookup on Login** - automatically loads your stats when you log in
- **Player Menu Lookup** - adds "Kill Clog" to right-click menus
- **Tooltip Activation** - hover or click-to-reveal
- **Cell Hover** - outline or tint feedback
- **Progress Highlighter** - color KC numbers by clog completion
- **Toggle Keybind** - shortcut to flip the highlighter on/off
- **Fully configurable colors** - completed, 1 away, in progress, empty, summary bar text
